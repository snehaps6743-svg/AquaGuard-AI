AquaGuard AI — English-translated Lake Biwa water-quality datasets

Source: Files supplied by the user, originally published by Shiga Prefecture, Japan.
Original data coverage: fiscal years 1979–2018 (40 annual records per parameter).
The original metadata describes annual average water quality at:
- North Lake / North Basin: 28 monitoring points
- South Lake / South Basin: 19 monitoring points
- Seta River: 2 monitoring points

Translated fields:
- Year: Gregorian year (西暦年度)
- Japanese_Era: Japanese era-year label (和暦年度), retained as supplied
- COD: Chemical Oxygen Demand (mg/L)
- Total_Nitrogen: Total nitrogen (mg/L)
- Total_Phosphorus: Total phosphorus (mg/L)
- North_Basin, South_Basin, Seta_River: annual average values for each region

Original data creator: Lake Biwa Conservation and Restoration Division (琵琶湖保全再生課).
Creation year shown in source metadata: 2019 (Reiwa 1).
Original source institutions listed: Kinki Regional Development Bureau, Ministry of Land,
Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism; Japan Water Agency; Shiga Prefectural Lake Biwa
Environmental Research Institute.

Important:
- Numerical measurements have been preserved; only labels/metadata were translated.
- These are annual averages, not real-time measurements.
- The three regional values are reported aggregates. Do not interpret them as individual
  monitoring-station readings.
- COD, total nitrogen, and total phosphorus are separate water-quality indicators.
- The combined file joins the three parameter files by year and Japanese era.
